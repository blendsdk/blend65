allowlist-of-one (`{shift 6}`) rather than arithmetic on `k`; coarsest-wins on conflicting demands;
a bare `&X` keeping 256; the mark staying at the `&` site rather than over IL operands; non-const
operands registering nothing ([#74](https://github.com/blendsdk/blend65/issues/74) owns the
resulting undiagnosed hazard); the mark becoming a value rather than a boolean plus a parallel set;
re-deriving the three fold-form oracles while the three `align-mixed` ones stay untouched as the
bare-`&` control; and acceptance criteria that never assert a corpus byte drop.

| 121 | Edge case (authoring) | RD-15 **AC-5** requires that `lo(&X / 65536)` — *"`k = 16`, rejected by the fold and lowered through the ordinary path"* — also keeps 256. **Measured against the real compiler: the program does not compile at all.** `65536` exceeds the 16-bit maximum, so the **lexer** rejects it with `E10216` (`packages/frontend/src/lexer/lexer.ts:238`, `NumericLiteralOverflow`) before any lowering happens: no assembly, no symbol map, no boundary. The other k=16 spelling, `lo(&X >> 16)`, is also a hard error — `W10174` then `E90001`, no emission — which RD-13 owns (AR #89) and its ST-13h already pins | (a) **Drop the `/ 65536` clause** from AC-5 and record why: there is no k=16 form that compiles, so the extremes AC-5 can actually cover are `k = 0` (`/ 1`) and `k = 15` (`/ 32768`), both measured green / (b) Replace it with `lo(&X >> 16)` and assert the diagnostics instead / (c) Keep the clause and assert that the program fails to build | ✅ Resolved — User accepted recommendation: **(a)**. (b) would be a diagnostics assertion wearing an alignment criterion's clothes, and it duplicates RD-13's ST-13h; (c) would put a lexer range check inside a placement criterion. AC-5's stated purpose — covering the extremes `foldedAddressByte` accepts — is fully served by `k = 0` and `k = 15`, since a fold-*rejected* k=16 operand is unreachable through any spelling. **Back-propagated** into RD-15's AC-5 and Spec-Test Inventory in Phase 3 | ✅ Resolved |
